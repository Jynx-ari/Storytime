# Elias

## Identity

**Full Name:** TBD  
**Public Name:** [[Alias]]  
**Role:** Billionaire / Industrialist / Power Broker

## Core Concept

Elias does not become a politician.

Instead, he becomes one of the most powerful private figures in the Philippines during the [[New Renaissance]].

His wealth and companies become so deeply connected to the country's technological and economic infrastructure that **the government needs him**.

He does not need to sit in the political chair.

He becomes the person behind it.

> **He doesn't take the chair. He makes the person sitting in it dependent on him.**

## Character Arc

### The One Who Stands

- Ordinary citizen
- Experiences society from below
- Sees the failures of bad governance firsthand
- Wants to change the system

### The Builder

- Enters the technological and economic world
- Creates or builds something important during the [[New Renaissance]]
- His success grows beyond what he expected
- His company becomes increasingly important to society

### The Billionaire

- Accumulates enormous wealth
- Expands into critical industries and infrastructure
- Becomes increasingly difficult for the government to replace
- Gains influence without holding public office

### The Second Hand

Elias becomes a power behind the government.

The government still exists.

Politicians still hold office.

Elections still happen.

But important parts of the state depend on Elias' companies, infrastructure, investment, technology, or expertise.

He can influence political decisions without being a politician himself.

### The One Behind the Chair

Elias does not become the person sitting in the chair.

He becomes the person who can determine who gets to sit there.

His power comes from **dependency**, not formal authority.

## Sources of Power

Potential areas controlled or heavily influenced by Elias:

- AI infrastructure
- Energy
- Communications
- Logistics
- Financial technology
- Government technology
- National data infrastructure
- Major private investment
- Other critical industries

These should be developed gradually rather than making Elias instantly powerful.

## Relationship With Government

The government may publicly insist that it is independent from Elias.

Privately, however, removing him could create severe economic and technological consequences.

This creates Elias' greatest leverage:

> **The government needs him more than it can afford to oppose him.**

## Relationship With Valeria

[[Valeria]]

Valeria is secretly connected to the [[Anti-Alias Group]].

Elias knows her as Valeria, but she may use the cover identity [[Esther]] when interacting with Alias.

Their relationship develops while Valeria is secretly connected to people working against Elias' public identity.

## Themes

- Power without formal authority
- Dependency as control
- Becoming what you originally opposed
- The difference between governing and controlling
- The danger of believing only you can fix the system
