## Core Concept

Elias begins as an ordinary person who experiences society from the perspective of **the one who stands**.

He grows up seeing the consequences of bad governance and develops a determination to change the system.

His story eventually becomes a tragedy of power: the person who wanted to end bad governance gradually becomes part of the very system he once hated.
