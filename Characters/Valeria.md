## Core Concept

Valeria is a journalist who operates publicly under the alias **Esther**.

The distinction between Valeria and Esther can represent two sides of her identity:

- **Valeria** — her personal identity
- **Esther** — her professional/journalistic identity

Her journalism eventually places her in conflict with Elias as his political power grows.

## Relationship with Elias

Valeria and Elias develop a close relationship.

At some point, Elias may know her personally as **Valeria** while also encountering her journalistic work under the name **Esther**.

The eventual discovery that Valeria and Esther are the same person can create a major personal and political conflict.
