# Valeria

## Identity

**Full Name:** TBD  
**Cover Name:** [[Esther]]  
**Role:** Journalist / Activist / Member of the [[Anti-Alias Group]]

## Core Concept

Valeria is a journalist who is secretly affiliated with a group opposed to [[Alias]], Elias' public identity.

She investigates and works against Alias while becoming personally close to Elias.

Her biggest secret is not that she is a journalist.

It is that **she is Valeria while Alias may know her as Esther**.

## Esther

**Esther** is Valeria's cover identity.

It is not her journalistic identity.

It exists specifically to conceal her connection to the people working against Alias.

The exact circumstances surrounding the creation and use of the Esther identity are TBD.

## The Anti-Alias Group

Valeria is affiliated with a broader opposition network made up of people who oppose Alias and his growing influence.

Possible members include:

- Activists
- Investigative journalists
- Whistleblowers
- Political dissidents
- Researchers
- Lawyers
- Former employees
- Government insiders
- Information specialists
- People affected by Alias' corporations

The exact structure and name of the group are TBD.

## Purpose of the Group

Possible objectives:

- Expose Alias
- Investigate his influence
- Reveal the government's dependence on him
- Limit his power
- Prevent private power from overtaking public institutions
- Gather evidence against him

## Relationship With Elias

Valeria and Elias develop a close personal relationship.

The irony is that Valeria is connected to people actively working against Alias.

Elias may trust Valeria personally while being unaware of her connection to the opposition.

At the same time, Valeria has to reconcile the man she knows with the powerful figure her group is fighting.

## Double-Life Dynamic

### Valeria

- Real identity
- Journalist
- Connected to the Anti-Alias Group
- Personally close to Elias

### Esther

- Cover identity
- Used when necessary to conceal Valeria's connection to the opposition
- Potentially known by people connected to Alias
- Separate from her ordinary professional identity

### The Conflict

Elias knows Valeria.

Alias is Elias.

Valeria knows both sides.

The question is whether Elias realizes that **Valeria and Esther are the same person**.
