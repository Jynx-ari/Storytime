# Alias

## Identity

**Real Name:** [[Elias]]  
**Public Name:** Alias  
**Associated Character:** [[Valeria]]

## Core Concept

Alias is the public identity associated with Elias as his influence grows.

He is not a politician.

He is a billionaire industrialist and power broker whose companies become so important to the country that the government increasingly depends on him.

## Public Image

Alias can be presented publicly as:

- Billionaire
- Industrialist
- Technology leader
- Philanthropist
- National development figure
- Major investor

His public image does not necessarily make him appear like a dictator.

He may instead appear to be the person who keeps the country moving.

## The Power Behind the Chair

Alias does not need formal political office.

His influence comes from the government's dependence on the infrastructure, technology, investment, and economic systems controlled by him.

> **He doesn't take the chair. He makes the person sitting in it dependent on him.**

## Government Dependency

The government may publicly insist that it is independent from Alias.

Privately, however, removing him could cause severe economic and technological consequences.

The government needs him more than it can afford to oppose him.

## Opposition

[[Valeria]] is connected to the [[Anti-Alias Group]].

The group seeks to expose, weaken, or stop Alias' growing influence.

Valeria uses [[Esther]] as a cover identity when necessary.

## Double-Life Conflict

Alias and Valeria can develop a personal relationship while unknowingly existing on opposite sides of the same conflict.

Alias may not initially know:

- Valeria is connected to his opposition
- Esther is Valeria
- She is helping investigate him

Valeria may not initially understand:

- How much power Elias actually possesses
- How deeply the government depends on him
- How far he is willing to go to preserve that position
