# Esther

## Identity

**Real Identity:** [[Valeria]]  
**Type:** Cover Identity  
**Affiliation:** [[Anti-Alias Group]]

## Core Concept

Esther is the cover identity used by [[Valeria]] while operating against [[Alias]].

Esther is **not** Valeria's journalistic identity.

Valeria remains a journalist.

Esther exists to conceal Valeria's connection to the [[Anti-Alias Group]] and to protect her real identity while interacting with people connected to Alias.

## Purpose

The Esther identity can be used to:

- Conceal Valeria's connection to the opposition
- Protect the Anti-Alias Group
- Investigate Alias without exposing her real identity
- Interact with people connected to Alias
- Maintain separation between Valeria's personal life and her activities against Alias

The exact purpose and circumstances surrounding the creation of Esther are TBD.

## Relationship With Valeria

Esther is an identity created and maintained by [[Valeria]].

Valeria is the real person.

Esther is the identity she presents when the situation requires her to conceal who she actually is.

This creates a distinction between:

**Valeria**
- Real identity
- Journalist
- Personal life
- Relationship with [[Elias]]

**Esther**
- Cover identity
- Connected to operations against [[Alias]]
- Used to conceal Valeria's affiliation
- Potentially known by people who must not know Valeria's real identity

## Relationship With Alias

Esther exists primarily because of [[Alias]].

She may come into contact with Alias' network while concealing her real identity.

The exact extent of Alias' knowledge about Esther is TBD.

Possible possibilities:

- Alias knows Esther only as a separate individual
- Alias has encountered Esther without realizing she is Valeria
- Alias becomes suspicious of Esther
- Alias eventually discovers that Esther is Valeria

## Relationship With Elias

[[Elias]] and Valeria develop a personal relationship.

The central irony is that Elias may know Valeria personally while encountering Esther separately.

If Elias does not initially know that:

> **Esther = Valeria**

then Valeria is effectively maintaining two identities in Elias' world.

This creates the possibility that Elias could trust Valeria while simultaneously investigating or becoming suspicious of Esther.
