# Story — Current Brain Dump

## Setting

- The setting is in the **Philippines**.
- The time period is around a new so-called **Renaissance** caused by the rise of AI and AI-related technology.
- The era slowly develops into something resembling a **pre-Cyberpunk** setting.
- This is before the heavily sci-fi technology of a full Cyberpunk era happens.
- The story is therefore set during the transition toward that future.

## Elias

- Elias is the main character.
- He lived a very hard life.
- He promised to end bad governance.
- He eventually becomes what he hated the most.
- Elias experiences both sides of society.

### The One Who Stands

- Elias is the person who **stands**.
- This connects to the idea/reference of *Commuting*.
- He represents the ordinary person and the person experiencing society from below.
- He stands among the people.

### The One Who Sits

- Elias eventually becomes the person who **sits**.
- This connects to the idea/reference of *Upuan*.
- He becomes the person in the chair: the person with political power and authority.
- The same person who once stood against bad governance eventually occupies the position of power he once hated.

## Elias / Alias

- The name **Elias** sounds similar to **alias**.
- This was noticed as an ironic connection.
- **Alias** could become Elias's future cover name.
- "Alias" represents the identity he takes on later in his life.
- This can create a distinction between **Elias** and the person he becomes as **Alias**.

## The Journalist

- There is a **female journalist**.
- The journalist begins as an enemy of Elias/Alias.
- The relationship can develop into **enemies to lovers**.
- She investigates Alias.
- She eventually becomes **Alias's mirror**.
- She represents another path that Elias could have taken.
- She can understand and challenge the difference between the person Elias was and the person he becomes.

## Core Character Idea

Elias lives both sides of the story:

> **The one who stands.**
>
> **The one who sits in the chair.**

He begins as the person who wants to change the system and eventually becomes part of the system he wanted to destroy.

## Ending Idea

- Elias eventually becomes good again.
- He dies after becoming good.
- His death should be part of the story's eventual conclusion.

## References / Inspirations Mentioned

- *Commuting* — associated with **standing** and the ordinary person's perspective.
- *Upuan* — associated with **sitting in the chair**, power, and authority.
- Cyberpunk — used as the eventual direction of the setting, but the story happens earlier, during the transition toward that era.
