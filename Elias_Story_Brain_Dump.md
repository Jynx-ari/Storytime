# Core Story

The setting is the Philippines during a fictional **New Renaissance** driven by AI and AI-related technology.

The country gradually transitions toward a **pre-Cyberpunk era**.

Elias is the main character.

He lived a very hard life and promised himself that he would end bad governance.

---

# New Direction for Elias

Instead of becoming a politician, Elias becomes a billionaire power.

He gains influence through technology, business, infrastructure, and wealth.

The government eventually **needs him**.

He becomes something like a second hand from which political power can be influenced.

He doesn't necessarily control politics directly.

Rather:

> **The government needs Elias to function.**

He doesn't have to sit in the political chair.

He can influence the person sitting in it.

# Main Irony

Elias wants to end bad governance.

But his solution creates another problem:

**The government becomes dependent on one private individual.**

He may start believing:

> "Someone has to keep the country functioning."

Then:

> "They keep making mistakes."

Then:

> "They need me."

Then:

> "If they need me anyway, why shouldn't I decide?"
